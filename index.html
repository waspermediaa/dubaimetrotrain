Dubai Metro Timings: Daily Schedules, First & Last Train Operations

The complete commuter reference guide to weekly service hours, peak train frequencies, late-night departure cutoffs, and airport transit schedules.

Synchronized Transit Operations: 99.8% on-time punctuality across Red and Green lines Source: RTA Transit Operations
Verified Operational Timetables · Updated for Regular & Seasonal Cycles

Dubai's reputation as a bustling international commerce and tourism hub is sustained by one of the most reliable and punctual urban rail networks in the world. Engineered and monitored by the Roads and Transport Authority (RTA), the autonomous transit system operates on precise headways that ensure consistent movement throughout the morning rush, business hours, and late-night social commutes. Because platform gate closures and train frequencies change depending on the day of the week, commuters who verify the real-time Dubai metro timings before planning their travel avoid missed platform interchanges, unexpected station gate closures, and unnecessary taxi expenses.

Following the nationwide transition to a Monday-to-Friday working week in 2022, the RTA standardized its transit timetables to accommodate earlier Friday traffic flows and extended weekend operating windows. Knowing the exact opening times, final train departures, and peak headway intervals is vital for seamless daily journey planning.

Essential Passenger Advice: Station entrance barriers typically stop admitting passengers approximately 5 to 10 minutes prior to the scheduled arrival of the final train. If you are catching the last train of the night from major transfer hubs like Union or BurJuman, aim to arrive on the platform at least 15 minutes before the posted departure cutoff.

1. Standard Weekly Operating Hours Breakdown

The Dubai Metro operates seven days a week across both the Red Line and the Green Line. While the system operates on identical core opening hours across most weekdays, Friday evenings and Sunday mornings follow distinct schedules:

Day of the Week First Train / Opening Last Train / Closing Service Window
Monday to Thursday 05:00 AM 12:00 Midnight 19 Continuous Hours
Friday 05:00 AM 01:00 AM (Next Day) 20 Extended Hours
Saturday 05:00 AM 12:00 Midnight 19 Continuous Hours
Sunday 08:00 AM 12:00 Midnight 16 Continuous Hours

A widespread misconception among tourists is that Friday operations still start late in the morning. Since January 2022, Friday metro services commence promptly at 5:00 AM, aligning with general weekday business routines across the emirate.

2. Understanding First and Last Train Departure Logistics

A common source of confusion for new passengers is interpreting what "closing at midnight" actually means. Midnight does not mean that all train wheels stop moving at 12:00 AM; rather, it indicates the time when the final train departs from the line's terminal stations:

  • Red Line Final Departures: The last continuous train heading from Centrepoint toward Expo 2020 or UAE Exchange typically departs between 11:20 PM and 11:35 PM on regular weekdays, reaching outer stations around midnight or 12:30 AM.
  • Green Line Final Departures: Trains leaving Etisalat by e& toward Creek station depart their initial platform around 11:30 PM, arriving at terminus facilities before the stroke of midnight.
  • Interchange Transfer Cutoffs: At Union and BurJuman, the final connecting trains crossing between the Red and Green lines normally arrive between 11:45 PM and 12:10 AM on standard weekdays (and up to 1:15 AM on Friday nights).

3. Train Headways: Peak vs. Off-Peak Frequencies

The automated signaling system dynamically modulates train dispatch intervals according to passenger demand. You do not need to memorize a rigid timetable during the daytime, as trains operate on tight headway intervals:

  • Morning Peak Hours (06:30 AM – 09:00 AM): Trains arrive on platforms every 2.5 to 3 minutes along the central Red Line trunk, ensuring swift crowd clearance along Sheikh Zayed Road.
  • Evening Rush Hours (05:00 PM – 08:00 PM): Dispatch intervals remain between 2.5 and 3.5 minutes to accommodate corporate departures heading northbound toward Deira, Sharjah borders, and airport corridors.
  • Off-Peak Daytime Windows: Mid-day services (10:00 AM to 04:00 PM) generally run every 4 to 6 minutes.
  • Late Evening & Weekend Evenings: After 10:00 PM and on Sunday mornings, intervals widen slightly to between 6 and 8 minutes.

4. Catching Flights: DXB Airport Metro Timings

Dubai International Airport terminals are seamlessly integrated into the Red Line via dedicated concourse stations at Terminal 1 (R14) and Terminal 3 (R13). For passengers organizing flight arrivals or departures, keeping schedule boundaries in mind is essential:

  1. Early Morning Departures: If you have an international flight boarding before 07:00 AM on weekdays or before 10:00 AM on Sundays, verify whether the first metro at 05:00 AM (08:00 AM on Sundays) provides sufficient time for baggage check-in and customs clearance.
  2. Red-Eye Flight Arrivals: International flights landing past 11:30 PM may touch down after the final southbound metro train has departed the airport concourse. In such instances, travelers should plan to utilize official RTA airport taxis or the 24-hour public bus network.

5. Extended Operating Hours During Holidays & Events

During significant public celebrations and religious observations, the RTA routinely updates and extends operating hours to prevent vehicular traffic gridlock:

  • New Year's Eve (December 31 – January 1): The metro typically operates non-stop for 40 to 48 consecutive hours, providing continuous round-the-clock connectivity between Downtown Burj Khalifa, Dubai Marina, and outer park-and-ride facilities.
  • Eid Al Fitr & Eid Al Adha: Operating hours are frequently extended to run until 02:00 AM or 03:00 AM across all holiday evenings.
  • The Holy Month of Ramadan: Monday to Saturday operational timings generally remain consistent, while Sunday schedules may expand slightly to accommodate late-night family gatherings and Suhoor dining across cultural quarters.

Summary

Mastering Dubai's urban rail network relies heavily on schedule awareness. By remembering the 5:00 AM weekday starts, the 8:00 AM Sunday morning schedule, and the extended 1:00 AM Friday night operating window, both residents and visitors can navigate the emirate with speed, comfort, and peace of mind.
